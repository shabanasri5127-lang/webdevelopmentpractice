About SkillBridge

SkillBridge helps students and early-career professionals bridge the gap between learning and meaningful work — through guided learning plans, mentor connections, practical projects and AI-powered career advice.

Our Mission

Make career growth accessible by combining hands-on projects, mentor guidance, and personalized AI coaching so learners can confidently apply for real roles.

What we value

  • Practical learning — building real projects that demonstrate ability.
  • Mentorship — human guidance to accelerate growth.
  • Equity — opportunities for all backgrounds.

Our story

Started by graduates and mentors, SkillBridge began as a community project to help students get internship-ready. Over time it grew into a platform combining mentor networks and lightweight AI assistance.
